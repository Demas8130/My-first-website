About KaziScout

KaziScout is an early prototype built in Kenya to help beginners find remote and online opportunities more safely.

We do not offer jobs directly. We guide you to official careers pages like Safaricom, KCB Group, Equity Bank, CloudFactory, and Upwork, so you can verify eligibility yourself.

Built by Ogolo Roy Demas in Langata Rongai, Kenya. Mission: reduce job scams by showing source, last-checked date, and official link for every listing.
